Lab & PC Management

How labs and PCs work: Each lab has a name and a PC count. PCs are numbered automatically — e.g. a lab named Lab 1 with count 10 generates Lab 1 – PC-01 through Lab 1 – PC-10. QR sticker dimensions are fixed at 160 × 160 px on screen and ~38 × 38 mm when printed on A4 (5 columns, 10 mm margins). Keep lab names short so they fit the sticker header.

Edit PC specs

Select a lab and PC, then fill in the hardware details and serial numbers. Leave a field blank to keep the built-in catalog default. Changes are saved in this browser only — to push them to all devices, edit the CATALOG object in core.js and redeploy.
